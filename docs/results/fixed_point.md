## Exploratory: deviation growth after Safe

Open-loop spectral radius of A is 1.1566, so the asymptotic rate is ln = 0.1455 per step.
Fitted log-slope of the measured deviation while Safe, 200 seeds: mean 0.1279, 95% bootstrap CI [0.1247, 0.1308], sd 0.0227.
The same fit applied to the exact prediction ||A^k e0||: mean 0.1279.
Largest gap between measured and exactly propagated log-deviation over every Safe frame of every seed: 6.95e-11.

Reading: the measured slope sits below ln(rho) because a finite window is dominated by the transient of the sub-dominant modes, not because fixed-point arithmetic changes the dynamics. The deviation after Safe is the entry deviation propagated by A, so this part of the experiment measures the plant, not the arithmetic.
